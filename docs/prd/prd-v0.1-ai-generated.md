# DefenSME PRD v0.1 — AI-generated baseline

**Status:** Superseded after human review

The initial AI draft proposed asset inventory, manual and JSON/CSV alert intake, Vietnamese alert explanations, severity recommendations, remediation checklists, human approval, incident assignment, dashboards, reports, and audit history. It excluded autonomous remediation, endpoint agents, antivirus, malware sandboxing, and enterprise SIEM capabilities.

The direction was suitable, but the draft was not implementation-ready: it lacked stable IDs, precise permissions, AI failure behavior, import safety, measurable quality targets, acceptance criteria, and traceability. Those defects are preserved here as course evidence and corrected in `prd-v1.0.md`.
