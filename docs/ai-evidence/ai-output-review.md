# AI output review

## Accepted

- Alert triage and incident guidance as the MVP core.
- Modular backend plus a provider-neutral AI boundary.
- Explicit accept/edit/reject and preservation of original/corrected output.
- Manual/file ingestion for the first end-to-end workflow.
- Auditability and degraded operation without AI.

## Corrected

- Split observed, recommended, and approved severity.
- Replaced generic approval with persisted review metadata.
- Added IDs, priorities, error paths, measurable targets, tenant isolation, import safety, and traceability.

## Rejected

- Autonomous isolation, blocking, deletion, or command execution.
- Full SIEM, antivirus, malware sandbox, endpoint agent, and custom model training.
- Unsupported market statistics or AI-accuracy claims.
